MILKY WAY — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

You can search MILKY WAY without creating an account or sharing GPS location. When you register an account or publish a service listing, we collect only the details you provide, including your display name, email address, phone/WhatsApp business contact details, business descriptions, photos, reviews, and direct messages.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to operate the MILKY WAY service directory, display published business profiles, enable messaging between customers and providers, moderate listings, prevent fraud and abuse, and provide customer support.

3. Location & Verification Privacy

GPS location is optional. Mobile service providers publish a general public service area and radius rather than a private home address. Identity and business verification documents are stored privately and visible only to you and moderation staff.

4. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell your personal information. Published business profiles, ratings, and reviews are publicly visible in the directory. Data is stored securely on encrypted cloud infrastructure with row-level access controls.

5. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You may update your profile or permanently delete your account at any time inside the app under Settings → Account → Delete account, or submit a request via our Account Deletion page. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, saved providers, and listings.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact our support team at your published support email.
