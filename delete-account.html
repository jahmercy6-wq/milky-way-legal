Delete Your MILKY WAY Account

Permanent self-service account & data deletion

Delete Immediately Inside the App

  1. Open the MILKY WAY app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Scroll to Privacy & security and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm Delete permanently.

What Data Is Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your authentication account, user profile, saved providers, and owned service listings immediately. Moderation audit entries required for platform safety and fraud prevention may be retained in anonymized form where required by law.

Request Deletion Without App Access

If you no longer have access to the app, email our support address from the email registered on your MILKY WAY account with the subject line Account Deletion Request. Requests are completed within 7 business days.
